Empty Legs.

One-way flights on aircraft that are repositioning. Often bookable last minute, sometimes at a lower price.

Get AlertsCall 954.481.0344

How it works

A flight that is already going.

When an aircraft needs to head home or reposition for its next trip, that leg flies without passengers. Those one-way flights can be booked.

The trade-off is flexibility: the route is tied to the aircraft’s schedule. Dates can often shift a little, and sometimes the aircraft can use a nearby executive airport instead.

  • One-way trips
  • Often last minute
  • Sometimes at a lower price than a full charter

A good fit when

Your plans can flex.

  • Flexible dates

    If a day or two either way works, more legs will match your trip.

  • One-way travel

    Ideal when you only need to get there, not back.

  • Last-minute trips

    Many legs open up only days before departure.
