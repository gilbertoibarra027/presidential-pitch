Our home at FXE

Built for a smooth departure.

About 36,000 sq ft of office, hangar and maintenance space at Fort Lauderdale Executive, with aircraft based across New Jersey, Pennsylvania, California, the Carolinas, Miami, Boca Raton and Fort Lauderdale.
