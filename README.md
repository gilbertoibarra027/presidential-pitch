## Home structure
1. Header: logo, Services dropdown (desktop) and burger menu (≤1180px), phone, Schedule Your Flight. 1.7s airplane-window intro.
2. Hero: looping AI video (`video/`). The poster is the LCP image and is preloaded; sources are injected after window `load`. Desktop or mobile variant is chosen by `(max-width: 768px), (orientation: portrait)`. The video is skipped under reduced motion or Save-Data, and pauses offscreen. If it fails, the poster falls back to the old Ken Burns motion. Booking bar (step 1) overlays it.
3. Trust strip · 4. Fleet · 5. Why Us + 4 perk images (AI placeholders, 3:2) · 5b. Our Services grid
6. Global: canvas globe that spins slowly and cycles 13 example routes from FXE (arc + label pill). It pauses offscreen and is static under reduced motion.
7. Empty Legs band → empty-legs.html · 8. Closing CTA + contact form + footer

## Shared
`css/style.css` and `js/main.js` are used by every page. Every `form[data-lead]` gets generic validation and a success state. On subpages, Schedule Your Flight opens a trip-request sheet.
